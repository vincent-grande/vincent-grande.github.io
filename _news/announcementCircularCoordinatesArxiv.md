A preprint of my paper [Selecting Interpretable Circular Coordinates from Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08230), joint with [Marina Meila](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/mmp/), is now available on arXiv! We introduce CIRCOL, a method for selecting scientifically meaningful circular coordinates that explain loop structure in data. This combines ideas from persistent cohomology to more classical dictionary learning, and in the end we show the convergence of an inner product on simplicial cochains to the continuous inner product on differential 1-forms. :tada:
